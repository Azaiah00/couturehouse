Recommended opener

Lead with the helpful observation

Hi, is this the owner or the person who handles the website for [Business]? My name is Fred Sales with Couture House. We build and refresh websites for Black-owned salons and independent stylists. I found your business online, and the website currently linked to it wasn’t loading when I checked. Are you already working on that, or would it be useful if I showed you a simple way to get it back online?

Why this works: it is specific, accurate, low-pressure, and ends with an easy diagnostic question.

Voicemail / gatekeeper

Keep the message under 25 seconds

Hi, this is Fred Sales with Couture House. I noticed the website linked to [Business] isn’t loading. We specialize in building and revamping websites for Black-owned salons and stylists. I can send a quick screenshot and a simple fix plan. Call or text me at [your number].

Avoid saying “your website is bad.” The public listing may be old or incorrect. Say exactly what you observed and let them explain.
