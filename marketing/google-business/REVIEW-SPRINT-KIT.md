# Couture House Google Review Sprint

Prepared: August 20, 2026

## Objective

Request honest Google reviews from five completed clients, receive two to three initial reviews, and secure separate permission to reuse approved quotes on the Couture House website and Instagram.

## Before Sending Anything

1. Complete Google Business Profile verification.
2. On a desktop browser, open the Business Profile.
3. Select `Read reviews` and then `Get more reviews`.
4. Copy the official Google review link.
5. Replace `[GOOGLE REVIEW LINK]` in the messages below.

Google currently allows businesses to request reviews based on genuine customer experiences. Do not offer gifts, discounts, free work, or other incentives. Do not request a particular rating, tell clients what to write, or ask only clients who promise to leave a positive review.

1. Beverly's of Nashville
2. The Dreadlocks Salon

4. Magic Coils

Confirm that each recipient was a genuine client or customer before sending a Google review request.

No pressure and no need to say anything specific—whatever genuinely reflects your experience is perfect. Here’s the direct link: [GOOGLE REVIEW LINK]

## One Follow-Up After Four Days

> Hey [Name] — just circling back on the Couture House review link in case it got buried. There’s no pressure at all, but your honest experience would help future business owners understand how we work: [GOOGLE REVIEW LINK]

Send only one follow-up. If the client does not respond, close the request without additional pressure.

## Thank-You and Reuse Permission

Send this only after the review is publicly posted:

> Thank you for taking the time to leave that review—it means a lot. Would you also be comfortable with Couture House featuring your exact words, your name, your business name, and a project image on our website and Instagram? We won’t change the meaning, and we’ll send you the final presentation if you’d like to approve it before publishing.

Record the answer separately. A public Google review should not automatically be treated as permission to reuse the client’s name, quote, logo, or project imagery in branded marketing.

## Five-Day Workflow

### Day 1

- Verify the profile and retrieve the official review link.
- Confirm the five clients and their preferred contact method.
- Send three personalized requests individually.

### Day 2

- Send the remaining two personalized requests.
- Record every request in `REVIEW-TRACKER.csv`.

### Day 3

- Reply to any new reviews from the Business Profile.
- Ask reviewers separately for marketing reuse permission.

### Day 5

- Send one polite follow-up to clients who have not responded.
- Close requests after the single follow-up.

## Reviews Highlight Publishing Gate

Do not publish the Reviews Highlight until at least two genuine reviews are available and reuse permission has been documented.

For each approved Story, collect:

- Exact review text—do not paraphrase it as a quote.
- Reviewer name and business name.
- Project or service delivered.
- Public review URL.
- Written permission to reuse the quote and attribution.
- Approved project image or website screenshot.
