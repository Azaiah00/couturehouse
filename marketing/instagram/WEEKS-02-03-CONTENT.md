## Faceless production system

Each Reel can be created from five layers:

1. AI-generated Couture House campaign art for the opener and transitions.
2. Real client website screen recordings.
3. Kinetic typography using the website's Arial/Georgia type system.
4. Cursor movement, scroll rhythm, and interface close-ups.
5. Couture House-owned or properly licensed sound.

For Higgsfield, use it only when motion adds meaning: camera movement through the generated digital-world artwork, hair-texture macro movement, glass-browser parallax, and polished transitions. Do not generate fake client outcomes, fake salon interiors presented as client work, or synthetic testimonials.

## Collaborator sequence

Invite a client as collaborator when the post centers their work:

- Beverly's of Nashville transformation.
- The Dreadlocks Salon website journey.
- All Things Locs website journey.
- Magic Coils commerce campaign.
- Photo Revival posts tied to the originating salon with permission.

Send the client the final asset and caption before publishing. Let them request factual corrections. A collaborator post can put the content in front of the client's existing audience, which is more valuable than a broad hashtag dump.
