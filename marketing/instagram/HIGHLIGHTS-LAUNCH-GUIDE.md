## Reviews — hold until proof is ready

Use `../google-business/REVIEW-SPRINT-KIT.md` and `../google-business/REVIEW-TRACKER.csv` to collect and document the first approved reviews.

Launch when there are at least two real reviews. Each Story should include:

- The client's exact words.
- Client name and business, with permission.
- The project or service delivered.
- A subtle project image or website frame.

Never create synthetic testimonials, paraphrase a client as a direct quote, or post private feedback without permission.

## Story design rules

- Use the existing Couture House Arial/Georgia system: heavy uppercase sans-serif for statements and italic Georgia for emotional emphasis.
- Keep essential text inside the central safe area; avoid the top and bottom interface zones.
- Use one message per Story.
- Add interactive stickers inside Instagram rather than baking fake controls into the image.
- Use link stickers only when the destination is specific and useful.
- Keep motion restrained: slow crops, browser scrolls, glass parallax, and type reveals.
- Save finished Stories to the correct Highlight immediately after publishing.
