# Couture House Highlight Cover Prompt Set

Initial set generated with the built-in image-generation tool on August 20, 2026. The Services and FAQ covers were added on August 21, 2026.

## Shared art direction

> Create a premium abstract editorial Instagram Story Highlight cover for Couture House, a faceless digital atelier serving Black-owned salons, hairstylists, locticians, and textured-hair brands. Square composition intended for a circular crop. Use one strong centered sculptural symbol with generous breathing room and keep all important detail inside the center 60 percent. Luxury cinematic 3D beauty-campaign art direction with photoreal smoked glass, polished obsidian, warm metal, and subtle braided-hair texture. Ink-black background, warm ivory inner glow, coral rim light, and one precise acid-lime accent. No text, letters, logos, people, faces, generic social-media icons, or watermark. The symbol must remain legible at very small size.

## Cover-specific symbols

- **Start Here:** a luminous sculptural portal or doorway emerging from darkness.
- **Work:** a floating editorial beauty-world assemblage combining sculptural coils, salon tools, polished hair forms, glass, and botanical accents.
- **Services:** six luminous smoked-glass and warm-copper service modules orbiting and connecting into one elegant core, with a subtle braided-cord structure and one acid-lime energy accent.
- **Websites:** a centered stack of translucent smoked-glass browser panes forming an elegant monolith, with subtle interface lines and no text.
- **The Glow Up:** one braided form crossing a vertical transformation seam, raw and shadowed on one side and polished/editorial on the other.
- **Reviews:** two elegant sculptural quotation forms suggested through curved smoked glass, surrounded by a subtle halo.
- **Process:** a graceful looping path made of smoked glass and braided cord connecting four luminous sculptural nodes.
- **FAQ:** two interlocking smoked-glass conversation forms joined by braided black cord and warm copper hardware, with one small acid-lime answer light.
