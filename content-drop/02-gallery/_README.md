# 02-gallery/

Wide landscape photos for the "Selected Frames" section on the homepage
(the one that scrolls horizontally).

## Spec

- **Aspect**: 16:10 or 3:2 landscape
- **Size**: ≥ 2400px wide, ideally ≥ 3000px
- **Format**: jpg or png (I'll convert to webp during integration)
- **Count**: 7 minimum, 10 ideal, more is fine — I'll pick the strongest

## What looks good

- Editorial campaign frames, lifestyle, behind-the-scenes
- Strong subject, clear focal point
- Variety across the set (don't drop 8 nearly-identical product shots)

## Filename rules

Anything goes. Suggestion for ordering: `01-…`, `02-…`, etc. — they'll display in alphabetical order.

Example:
```
01-spring-campaign.jpg
02-studio-portrait.jpg
03-product-detail.jpg
...
```
