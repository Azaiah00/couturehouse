# 07-showreel/

The wide secondary video that sits between the Manifesto and the Photo Gallery
on the homepage.

## Spec

- **Aspect**: 21:9 ideal (cinematic widescreen), 16:9 acceptable
- **Length**: 10–30 seconds
- **Format**: mp4 / h.264
- **Audio**: muted-friendly (it autoplays muted)
- **Size**: ≥ 1920px wide

## Filename

`showreel.mp4` (or `.mov`, `.webm`)

## What works

- A montage of your strongest work — campaign frames, product, motion
- Smooth, slow cuts (3–5 seconds per scene)
- Strong opening frame (it's also the still that shows before play starts)
