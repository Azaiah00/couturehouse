# Couture House 90-Day Content Calendar

## Publishing system

Minimum sustainable cadence:

- Instagram: three feed posts per week, five Story days per week.
- TikTok: three native posts per week.
- Website: one useful article or case-study update per week.
- Email: one useful note every other week for the first 90 days.
- Founder/team presence: at least one face-to-camera post per week.

The same idea can be adapted across channels, but do not upload a watermarked TikTok to Instagram or publish identical captions everywhere.

### Weekly rhythm

| Day | Asset | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | 20–35 second Reel/TikTok | Identify a painful digital problem |
| Wednesday | Screen-recorded teardown, build demo, or carousel | Teach the fix and demonstrate expertise |
| Friday | Case study, founder point of view, or offer | Create trust and invite a conversation |
| Tuesday/Thursday | Stories | Polls, questions, behind-the-scenes, proof, and DMs |
| Weekly | Search article | Capture the same problem in Google and AI search |

## Creative rules

### Short-form structure

1. **0–2 seconds:** visual contradiction or direct statement.
2. **2–7 seconds:** make the cost of the problem clear.
3. **7–25 seconds:** show the example, teardown, or fix.
4. **25–35 seconds:** show proof or the better experience.
5. **Final frame:** one CTA only.

### Visual language

- Open on the salon’s social page, broken site, mobile booking path, or finished transformation—not a logo animation.
- Use large on-screen text readable without sound.
- Keep the most important text out of platform UI zones.
- Use Couture House’s original music whenever rights permit; make several recognizable short sonic signatures.
- Favor screen recordings, hands-on-keyboard shots, sketching, before/after layouts, and real owner conversations.
- Use client content only with written permission.

### CTA rotation

- “DM `CROWN` for the scorecard.”
- “Comment `AUDIT` and we’ll send the checklist.”
- “Save this before your next website redesign.”
- “Send this to a stylist whose booking link is doing too much.”
- “Book a 20-minute fit call through the link in bio.”

Do not use a sales CTA on every post. A healthy mix is 60% education, 25% proof/personality, and 15% direct offer.

## Caption template

**Searchable first line:**  
“Salon website design should make booking easier—not add another place for clients to get lost.”

**Value:**  
Name the problem, show the consequence, explain one useful fix.

**Proof:**  
Reference a real project, test, screenshot, or client observation.

**CTA:**  
One next step.

Use the words salon owners actually search for in the spoken audio, on-screen text, caption, and title. Avoid stuffing captions with repetitive keywords.

## Instagram audience-growth routine

Five days per week:

1. Add 10–15 qualified accounts to the prospect tracker.
2. Follow no more than the accounts that genuinely fit the ICP.
3. Read the profile, booking link, reviews, and website before engaging.
4. Leave three to five specific comments that prove a human looked at the work.
5. Reply to one or two relevant Stories with a real question or observation.
6. Save useful posts and organize prospects by city or segment.
7. Send outreach only after a meaningful interaction or a clear buying signal.

Do not mass-follow, mass-like, use engagement bots, or send copy-paste pitches. Platforms can treat rapid, repetitive actions as spam, and the behavior damages the brand even when an account is not restricted.

### Discovery sources

- Instagram search, location tags, and relevant local salon accounts.
- TikTok search and Creator Search Insights.
- Google Maps salon and stylist results.
- StyleSeat, GlossGenius, Booksy, Vagaro, Square, and Fresha public profiles.
- ByBlack and local Black-business directories.
- Cosmetology schools, salon suites, conferences, and educator accounts.
- Existing client followers and collaborators—without scraping private information.

## TikTok operating notes

- Use a Business Account if the primary goal is promoting Couture House. TikTok says Business Accounts can add a website link and access Business Suite features, analytics, leads tools, and the Commercial Music Library.
- Use original Couture House music only when the business owns the necessary rights.
- Use Creator Search Insights weekly to find searched topics and content gaps.
- Review search analytics, watch time, rewatches, shares, profile visits, and website clicks.
- Prefer clear, human explanations over highly polished brand films for most educational posts.

## Weekly content scorecard

Track:

- Posts published.
- Qualified reach: salon/stylist accounts reached where observable.
- Average watch time and completion rate.
- Saves and shares.
- Profile visits.
- Website clicks.
- `CROWN` or `AUDIT` DMs/comments.
- Scorecards completed.
- Calls booked.
- Proposals and closed revenue.

Keep a winning concept when it creates conversations, not merely views. Recut the hook, example, and length before abandoning a useful topic.
