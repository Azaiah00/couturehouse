# Couture House Growth Strategy

This folder contains the working plan for making Couture House discoverable to Black-owned salons, stylists, and textured-hair brands and converting that attention into qualified business.

## First seven actions

1. Confirm the target geography and Google Business Profile eligibility.
2. Choose the primary 90-day offer and minimum price.
3. Record social, website, and sales baselines.
4. Install analytics, Search Console, a CRM, and calendar tracking.
5. Collect permission and results from existing salon/hair clients.
6. Build the Salon Digital Presence Scorecard.
7. Publish the Beverly’s case study and salon website service page.

## Strategy in one sentence

Turn Couture House’s cultural fluency, real salon work, and technical range into a proof-led demand engine: useful salon education creates attention; specific search pages and case studies create trust; a low-friction audit creates conversations; productized offers create sales.
