# Couture House Inbound Growth Plan

Prepared: August 16, 2026  
Execution week: August 17–23, 2026  
Primary goal: reposition Couture House as the specialist digital studio for Black-owned salons, salon owners, hair stylists, locticians, and textured-hair brands, then turn proof-led content into qualified inbound conversations.

## Executive decision

For the next 90 days, Couture House's acquisition message should be narrow even though its capabilities remain broad:

> Couture House builds websites, booking experiences, and content systems for Black-owned salons and hair stylists who want to get found, earn trust, and turn attention into appointments.

The business should pause broad fashion, general-contractor, podcast, and generic creative-agency messaging on the main Instagram feed. Those projects can remain in the portfolio, but salon and hair-business proof should lead the account.

### What is working

- The handle and visual identity are polished and consistent with the website.
- The website link is present.
- Existing Reels show that Couture House can produce high-quality visual content.
- Several posts use direct response calls to action such as DM keywords and link-in-bio prompts.

### What is blocking inbound salon leads

1. **The bio does not identify the buyer.** A salon owner cannot tell immediately that the account is for them.
2. **The feed and the website tell different stories.** The website is clearly focused on Black-owned salons, stylists, and hair-care brands, while most Instagram posts focus on fashion models, clothing brands, a contractor transformation, or a podcast.
3. **The best proof is missing from the grid.** Magic Coils, Beverly's of Nashville, The Dreadlocks Salon, All Things Locs, Washington WizKids, and the photo-revival before/after work should dominate the account.
4. **There is no visible salon-specific starting point.** The profile needs three pinned posts that explain the niche, show proof, and offer a simple next step.
5. **The current CTA is inconsistent.** Posts rotate between link in bio, DM, and comment keywords for unrelated offers. The next 30 days should use one primary CTA: DM `AUDIT` for a free three-point salon website review.
6. **There is little visible trust proof.** The grid needs client quotes, project context, process, and specific outcomes where clients can substantiate them.
7. **The account is too small for passive posting alone.** Publishing must be paired with daily, genuine participation in salon-owner conversations and client collaboration posts.

## Content system for the next 30 days

Publish three times per week. Use this mix:

- 40% proof: before/after work, case studies, client feedback, project decisions.
- 35% education: booking friction, website red flags, Google visibility, reviews, and ownership of the customer journey.
- 15% founder point of view: why the niche matters, how Couture House works, and what it believes.
- 10% direct offer: free audit, fit call, or limited project availability.

Recurring series:

- **Crowned Online:** salon and stylist website transformations.
- **Book or Bounce?:** quick mobile booking-journey reviews.
- **Website Red Flag:** one costly problem and one practical fix.
- **Behind the Build:** strategy, design, development, photo revival, and launch decisions.
- **The Digital Chair:** short conversations with salon owners about growth and operations.

Every post should answer at least one salon-owner question:

- Will this help me get found?
- Will this make me look more trustworthy?
- Will this make booking easier?
- Will this save me or my team time?
- Will this help me sell services or products?

## Review and social-proof sprint

### Google Business Profile status

Couture House previously created its Google Business Profile and reached Google's video-verification step. The business operates primarily remotely but also meets clients in person as part of delivering its services. Resume the existing profile rather than creating a duplicate.

Manage Couture House as a service-area business if clients are visited or met for genuine in-person services but are not received at the business address. Use the real business name, a real phone number, the authoritative website, accurate hours, and the smallest accurate category set. Hide the address when clients are not served there. Do not use a virtual-office, coworking, mailbox, or borrowed address as a storefront.

Recommended primary category to evaluate: `Website designer`. Possible secondary category: `Marketing agency` only if it accurately describes the main service.

For the verification video, create one continuous, unedited mobile recording through the Business Profile. Show:

1. Location evidence near the operating address, such as the street name, building number, or recognizable nearby landmark.
2. Evidence that Couture House exists, such as the workspace, computer and design tools, branded website, business cards, branded materials, or project-production equipment.
3. Evidence that Fred manages the business, such as access to business-only systems or a non-sensitive invoice, business registration, utility bill, or other document bearing the same Couture House name used on the profile.

Do not show private financial information, identification numbers, or other people's faces. The video must be recorded and uploaded from the mobile verification flow; Google does not permit a separately edited video to be uploaded later.

After verification:

1. Create the direct review link and QR code.
2. Request reviews from 5–8 real completed clients individually.
3. Do not offer discounts, gifts, or incentives.
4. Respond to every review with a short, specific reply.
5. Ask permission to reuse the best review on the website and Instagram.

### Review request message

> Hey [Name] — I’m building out Couture House’s client proof and I’d really appreciate an honest review of our work together. If you’re comfortable, could you mention what we created, what the experience was like, and what changed for your business? Here’s the direct link: [review link]. No pressure at all, and thank you again for trusting us with your project.

Do not give clients a prewritten review to paste. The prompt should help them describe their genuine experience in their own words.
